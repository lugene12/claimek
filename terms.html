2. What Claimek is — and is not

Claimek is a helper, not a judge. It is not a fact-checking organisation and does not publish fact-checks of its own. It does two things: it passes on ratings published by fact-checking groups, naming the group and linking to their article, and where no group has covered a claim, it shows an automated assessment of how likely the claim is to be inaccurate.

Automated checks can be wrong. Flags marked "Automated check" are produced by a computer, not by a person who checks facts for a living. They are worded as doubt, not as findings of fact, and they can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date. Nothing Claimek shows is medical, legal, money, or voting advice, and none of it is a final ruling on any claim, any video, or anyone's honesty. For anything important, check the original source yourself.

Claimek flags claims, not people. A flag is about a statement that appeared in a caption. It is not a statement about the person who said it, the channel that published it, or their honesty or motives.

If Claimek does not flag a claim, that does not mean it is true. Claimek can miss claims, read captions wrongly, or be down.

If a flag is wrong, tell us. Every flag carries a "Report this flag" link. We review reports, correct or remove flags we find to be wrong, and clear the saved result so the mistake is not repeated. You can also write to us at the address in section 12.
